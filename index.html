Questions

Do you work internationally?

Yes. AFAR works with brands and talent across markets, with a global network of creators, media and industry partners.

Do you only work with influencers?

No. Our work spans talent management, media partnerships, creative campaigns, events, affiliate marketing and social commerce.

Absolutely. We can work with your existing network or source additional talent through our wider network.

Can you manage an entire campaign?
